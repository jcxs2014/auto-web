Anomaly detection, the identification of collision events that deviate from Standard Model expectations, is one of the most promising model-agnostic strategies for new-physics searches at the Large Hadron Collider. Searching for rare events in learned low-dimensional representations removes the need to commit to hand-crafted observables a priori, but sacrifices the immediate physical interpretation those observables provide. For anomaly detection to enter a discovery pipeline, flagged events must therefore be diagnosed locally, both to distinguish potential new physics from mismodeling or detector effects and to identify observables for targeted follow-up searches. We evaluate supervised and self-supervised neural embedding strategies on COLLIDE-2V, a 750-million-event dataset simulated under realistic High-Luminosity LHC conditions, using the reconstruction error of an autoencoder trained in the learned latent space as the anomaly score. Across the configurations and benchmark signals considered, supervised objectives, which exploit simulation process labels during training, generally outperform their self-supervised counterparts and, in their best configurations, improve on an autoencoder trained directly on kinematic features, raising the signal efficiency at 10% background acceptance on all held-out processes. We further introduce a local diagnostic pipeline that fits a Gaussian Mixture Model to the SM latent space, profiles each component through high-level observables without process labels, localizes the flagged events, and ranks observables by their local discriminating power. The ranking recovers the defining kinematics of the held-out processes, while correlating the anomaly score with the same observables quantifies how closely the score tracks them, revealing cases where the most discriminating observable is not the one the score follows most closely.

异常检测，即识别偏离标准模型期望的碰撞事件，是大型强子对撞机新物理搜索中最有前途的模型不可知策略之一。在学习的低维表示中搜索罕见事件消除了先验手工制作的可观测值的需要，但牺牲了这些可观测值提供的直接物理解释。为了使异常检测进入发现流程，必须在本地诊断标记的事件，既可以将潜在的新物理与错误建模或探测器效应区分开来，又可以识别可观察到的目标后续搜索。我们使用在学习的潜在空间中训练的自动编码器的重建误差作为异常分数，在 COLLIDE-2V（一个在真实高亮度 LHC 条件下模拟的 7.5 亿个事件数据集）上评估监督和自监督神经嵌入策略。在所考虑的配置和基准信号中，在训练期间利用模拟过程标签的监督目标通常优于自监督目标，并且在最佳配置下，改进了直接在运动学特征上训练的自动编码器，在所有保留过程中以 10% 的背景接受度提高了信号效率。我们进一步引入了一个本地诊断管道，该管道将高斯混合模型拟合到 SM 潜在空间，通过没有过程标签的高级可观察量来分析每个组件，本地化标记的事件，并根据其本地区分能力对可观察量进行排名。该排名恢复了保留过程的定义运动学，同时将异常分数与相同的可观测值相关联，量化了分数跟踪它们的紧密程度，揭示了最具辨别力的可观测值并不是分数最接近的可观测值的情况。

We construct a geometric theory on the brane space $S^2M=C^\infty(S^2,M)$ associated to a gerbe with connection on $M$. Normalized logarithmic holonomy around the latitude circles defines an average holonomy line bundle on the brane space, whose connection and averaged flux give an equivariantly flat superconnection. We prove that the completed periodic exotic twisted cohomology defined by this superconnection localizes to the twisted cohomology of $M$. This {\bf brane space localization} happens in two stages: circle localization lands on the free path space of $M$, and contracting the paths, after a correction of the twisting form, brings us back to $M$. Parallel transport along the meridians lets us average gerbe module connections, after which the circular iterated-integral construction applies. The resulting {\bf twisted brane Bismut-Chern forms} restrict to the twisted Chern forms on $M$; their variation is described by {\bf twisted brane Bismut-Chern-Simons forms.} For a T-dual pair, transporting the classical Hori morphisms through localization yields {\bf graded genus-0 T-duality} on brane cohomology. The composite of each morphism with its dual is minus the Euler operator in the level variable, so these morphisms are isomorphisms at every nonzero level. At level one these morphisms are compatible with the brane character and twisted $K$-theory T-duality.

We discuss General Relativity and some of its modifications as effective field theories of the breaking of the affine general linear group using the coset approach and the Maurer-Cartan form. While the ultimate goal is to break the symmetry down to the Lorentz subgroup, we argue that there are three interesting breaking cascades in which the affine general linear group is first broken to a larger subgroup that contains the Lorentz group. In all cases, the breaking of translations always generates Goldstone modes that can be interpreted as metric degrees of freedom. We argue that the effective field theories that emerge as a result of the different breaking patterns are all dynamically equivalent at the lowest order of the derivative expansion, e.g., using Einstein-Hilbert-like actions, but can differ beyond the leading order.

我们讨论广义相对论及其一些修改，作为使用陪集方法和毛雷尔-嘉当形式破除仿射广义线性群的有效场论。虽然最终目标是将对称性分解为洛伦兹群，但我们认为存在三个有趣的分解级联，其中仿射一般线性群首先被分解为包含洛伦兹群的更大子群。在所有情况下，平移的破坏总是会产生可以解释为公制自由度的戈德斯通模式。我们认为，由于不同断裂模式而出现的有效场论在导数展开的最低阶上都是动态等效的，例如，使用类似爱因斯坦-希尔伯特的作用，但可以在主导阶之外有所不同。

We describe a family of two-dimensional metric deformations that preserve integrability. The seeds are (classically) conformal integrable field theories and the deformations are directly related to different classes of auxiliary field sigma models. We introduce a master action connecting deformations by Lie-algebra-valued auxiliary fields, the so-called $ν$-frame, to deformations by two Lorentz-charged scalars. These auxiliary scalar fields can be identified as Beltrami differentials of an auxiliary two-dimensional metric, providing a direct connection with the metric approach to $T\bar{T}$-like deformations. For Weyl-invariant seed matter theories, the auxiliary metric sector is described by a reduced gravitational potential. We show that the resulting framework preserves classical Lax integrability whenever the seed matter theory admits a Lax representation of its equations of motion on a generic curved two-dimensional spacetime. Finally, within the Lorentz-invariant family and the zweibein deformations of conformal matter theories considered here, we identify $T\bar{T}$ as the only deformation that can be interpreted as a field-dependent coordinate transformation.

We introduce a discrete spectral curve from $\mathscr{N}=2$ double-scaled SYK whose topological recursion defines quasi-polynomial volumes in integer boundary lengths. The curve has two edge theories: a local $\mathscr{N}=2$ theory at the lower edge and a bosonic theory at the upper edge. Focusing on the local $\mathscr{N}=2$ theory, we compute its finite-$q$ volumes and independently check the first few using the string equation. In the $q\rightarrow 1$ continuum limit, these recover the super Weil-Petersson volumes of Turiaci and Witten. Finally, we give an all-genus intersection-theoretic interpretation of these local volumes on the moduli space of stable marked curves, in terms of a deformed spin class with a gap-dependent $κ$-class dressing.

We establish that bond Hamiltonian densities, up to local difference terms whose sum vanishes under periodic boundary conditions, lying in the commutant of bond operators, $w_j\pm w_{j+1}$, is a necessary and sufficient condition for a one-dimensional nearest-neighbour Hamiltonian to conserve a uniform or staggered sum of the one-site operators $w_j$. Starting from these local commutants, we show that every such Hamiltonian and its conserved charge can be obtained from monodromy matrices constructed using regular and non-regular members of scalene Yang-Baxter triples. The results hold on periodic chains of length $L\geq3$, with even length required in the staggered case. The results do not require translation invariance, and hence the interaction on each bond can be chosen independently from the full corresponding commutant. Our representation-independent construction applies in arbitrary finite local dimension and exhausts the Hamiltonians with either prescribed additive symmetry, without requiring ordinary Yang-Baxter integrability. We illustrate the construction with spin-$\tfrac12$ and spin-1 systems and discuss its relation to the double-commutant theorem. Classifying the one-site generators $w$ and their associated bond commutants provides a route to classifying such Hamiltonians with additive conserved charges.

我们建立了键哈密顿密度，直到局部差分项，其总和在周期性边界条件下消失，位于键算子的交换子 $w_j\pm w_{j+1}$ 中，是一维最近邻哈密顿量保存单点算子 $w_j$ 的均匀或交错和的充分必要条件。从这些局部交换子开始，我们证明了每个这样的哈密顿量及其守恒电荷都可以从使用不等边斜角杨-巴克斯特三元组的规则和非常规成员构造的单向矩阵中获得。结果在长度为 $L\geq3$ 的周期链上成立，在交错情况下需要偶数长度。结果不需要平移不变性，因此每个键上的相互作用可以独立于完全相应的交换子进行选择。我们的与表示无关的构造适用于任意有限局部维度，并用任一规定的加性对称性耗尽哈密顿量，而不需要普通的杨-巴克斯特可积性。我们用 spin-$\tfrac12$ 和 spin-1 系统说明了构造，并讨论了它与双交换定理的关系。对单点生成元 $w$ 及其相关的键交换子进行分类，提供了一种对具有加性守恒电荷的哈密顿量进行分类的途径。

Collisionless shocks confine the particles they accelerate with the upstream wave field they themselves drive. Streaming particles resonantly amplify Alfvén waves, and the waves then confine the same particles by pitch-angle scattering. The rigidity a shock precursor can hold is therefore capped by how fast those waves grow, and that coupling has not previously been constrained in-situ at any interplanetary shock. Here we report Parker Solar Probe measurements of a fast, near-parallel coronal mass ejection-driven shock at 0.24~AU on 2023 March 13 that constrain this wave-particle coupling through the energetic-particle population it accelerates. We measure the spectral folding energy, the boundary between the confined and the upstream-escaping protons, and find that it falls from $\sim$6 to $\sim$1.5~MeV as $E_\mathrm{fold}\proptoΔt^{\,α}$ with $α\simeq0.34$, where $Δt$ is the time remaining before shock arrival. One precursor convective time before arrival this boundary is at $E_\mathrm{c}\simeq 2.46$~MeV, a characteristic confinement energy of a precursor of scale $L_\mathrm{EP}$. Read through the quasi-linear marginal-stability condition, the same evolution implies a resonant wave-growth timescale $τ_\mathrm{growth}(E)\propto E^{1/α}\simeq E^{2.92}$ that exceeds the precursor convective time above $E_\mathrm{c}$. The post-shock pitch-angle anisotropy reverses sign across the same confinement--escape transition at 2--5~MeV. The rigidity to which a shock precursor can confine particles is therefore set, in-situ, by how quickly the streaming population builds its own confining waves.

无碰撞激波将其加速的粒子限制在它们自身驱动的上游波场中。流动的粒子共振放大阿尔文波，然后波通过俯仰角散射限制相同的粒子。因此，激波前兆可以保持的刚性受到这些波增长速度的限制，并且这种耦合以前在任何行星际激波中都没有被限制在原位。在此，我们报告了帕克太阳探测器于 2023 年 3 月 13 日对一次快速、近乎平行的日冕物质抛射驱动的 0.24~AU 激波的测量，该激波通过其加速的高能粒子群限制了这种波粒耦合。我们测量了光谱折叠能量，即受限质子和上游逃逸质子之间的边界，发现它从 $\sim$6 下降到 $\sim$1.5~MeV，即 $E_\mathrm{fold}\proptoΔt^{\,α}$ 和 $α\simeq0.34$，其中 $Δt$ 是激波到达之前的剩余时间。到达此边界之前的一个前兆对流时间为 $E_\mathrm{c}\simeq 2.46$~MeV，这是 $L_\mathrm{EP}$ 规模的前兆的特征限制能量。通读准线性边际稳定性条件，相同的演化意味着共振波增长时间尺度 $τ_\mathrm{growth}(E)\propto E^{1/α}\simeq E^{2.92}$ 超过 $E_\mathrm{c}$ 以上的前兆对流时间。激波后俯仰角各向异性在 2--5~MeV 处的相同约束-逃逸转变中反转符号。因此，激波前兆限制粒子的刚性是由流动的群体建立自己的限制波的速度决定的。

Black holes are usually described either as classical geometries on which quantum fields propagate or as objects whose complete quantum description lies beyond semiclassical gravity. Hawking radiation already shows, however, that a black hole is not an exactly isolated system: the horizon carries a thermal scale and, after coarse graining, may be associated with fluctuations, dissipation and environmental correlations. Whether this horizon-scale openness can be encoded directly in the gravitational background, before any stochastic treatment of the metric, remains an open question. Here we answer it by introducing, for the first time, a conserved Hawking-scale anisotropic source representing the coarse-grained near-horizon atmosphere and solving the Einstein equations for a static black hole at fixed ADM mass. The dressed geometry has a renormalized mass function, a self-consistently shifted horizon and a modified surface gravity. Because the same lapse governs the horizon and timelike geodesics, the thermal response is accompanied by a correlated orbital restructuring: the effective potential is renormalized, the ISCO moves inward and the near-horizon residence time increases. The construction adds no Langevin force to a fixed Schwarzschild background; rather, it defines a deterministic dressed mean geometry, providing a controlled background on which stochastic metric fluctuations may subsequently be treated in the Einstein-Langevin framework. This establishes the deterministic mean-field sector as the necessary first step for future stochastic gravity programs addressing horizon-scale openness.

黑洞通常被描述为量子场在其上传播的经典几何形状，或者被描述为其完整量子描述超出半经典引力的物体。然而，霍金辐射已经表明，黑洞并不是一个完全孤立的系统：地平线带有热尺度，经过粗粒化后，可能与波动、耗散和环境相关性有关。在对度量进行任何随机处理之前，这种地平线尺度的开放性是否可以直接在引力背景中编码，仍然是一个悬而未决的问题。在这里，我们通过首次引入代表粗粒近地平线大气的守恒霍金尺度各向异性源并求解固定 ADM 质量的静态黑洞的爱因斯坦方程来回答这个问题。修饰后的几何体具有重整化的质量函数、自洽移动的地平线和修改的表面重力。因为同样的偏差控制着地平线和类时测地线，所以热响应伴随着相关的轨道重组：有效势被重新归一化，ISCO向内移动，近地平线停留时间增加。该结构没有向固定的史瓦西背景添加朗之万力；相反，它定义了一个确定性的修饰平均几何，提供了一个受控背景，随后可以在爱因斯坦-朗之万框架中处理随机度量波动。这将确定性平均场扇区作为未来解决地平尺度开放性的随机重力计划必要的第一步。

Collisionless shocks accelerate particles at a rate set by the diffusion coefficient of those particles in waves they themselves generate. Theory fixes that coefficient only in steady state, and no measurement has tested whether an evolving shock ever reaches that state. We report Parker Solar Probe observations of a fast, near-parallel interplanetary shock at 0.24 au, encountered four hours after eruption, with the upstream waves and the protons they scatter recorded on the same magnetic field line. From the wave spectra alone we derive the parallel mean free path of 0.05-30 MeV protons, resolved in energy and in distance from the shock. The mirror force of the measured compressive fluctuations supplies the scattering through the pitch angles that gyroresonance cannot reach. The mean free path grows with distance nearly as the steady self-generated solution requires, yet the steady balance holds at one energy only, near 1.3 MeV. The intensity profiles are a factor of two shallower than the measured coefficient predicts, because the waves are still growing. Confinement fails between 2.3 and 6 MeV, beginning a factor of two below what the age of the shock allows. The growth of the waves its own particles drive, rather than its age or its size, limits the energy an evolving shock confines.

Earth's ionosphere is primarily generated by the Sun's ionization of atoms in the upper atmosphere. This plasma environment is highly dynamic, exhibits strong spatial and temporal, variations and significantly impacts power systems, navigation, and satellite and ground communications. The University of Delaware Ionospheric Probe (UDIP) was developed as a hands-on learning experience for University of Delaware students on the ionosphere itself and on the development of space technology. Students (mostly undergraduates) have led all aspects of the experiment including mission planning, electronics development, mechanical development, software development, and scientific data analysis. The present study is intended as a guide to readers on how the development of space and near-space flight missions can be successfully incorporated into an undergraduate physics curriculum. The fourth version of the experiment (UDIP-4) flew along with student experiments from across the USA on the 2024 RockSat-C sounding rocket, which was launched from NASA Wallops Flight Facility. During the flight, UDIP-4's Langmuir probe successfully measured the ionosphere's density profile -- electron density as a function of altitude -- up to an apogee of 114 km. Though the measured density profile agrees well overall with the predictions of theoretical models, UDIP-4 identified a thin layer of enhanced electron density at an altitude of approximately 110 km that is consistent with a sporadic E layer.

SRAM-based FPGA system-on-chip devices are vulnerable to single-event upsets (SEUs) in configuration memory, which may perturb programmable routing resources and degrade communication fabrics. In modern Zynq UltraScale+ MPSoCs, such routing disturbances can introduce small propagation delay shifts that remain logically transparent yet compromise AXI-based data transfers and lead to silent data corruption. Although routing delay degradation and AXI interconnect failures have been studied independently, their experimental correlation under neutron irradiation has not been established. This work presents a cross-layer investigation on a ZCU104 platform integrating routing-dominated delay sensors with an AXI interconnect benchmark comprising replicated accelerators. Measured routing delay events are statistically correlated with communication failures, and cross-sections for both timing shifts and AXI malfunctions are derived. In this paper we propose a geometrically motivated formulation of gravity in an invariant Weyl--Integrable space-time (IWIST), in which the background geometry is not imposed a priori but is dynamically determined through the Palatini variational principle. For a scalar field non-minimally coupled to gravity action, the resulting compatibility condition defines a Weyl--Integrable geometry which is preserved by a local Weyl transformation of the metric and the geo­me­tri­cal scalar field. We construct an action invariant under both diffeomorphisms and Weyl transformations and introduce a Weyl-covariant variational procedure based on an invariant extension of the divergence theorem. The corresponding field equations are obtained for the metric, the Weyl scalar, and the Weyl gauge field. We further formulate the theory in the Einstein--Riemann frame, where the effective metric is Riemannian and the scalar field is re­inter­pre­ted as a phy­si­cal degree of freedom of geometric origin. Finally, we propose a geometrical mechanism for the explicit breaking of Weyl symmetry by introducing a coupling between the Weyl gauge field and a current constructed from the scalar sector. The resulting theory remains diffeomorphism covariant while departing from local Weyl invariance. The Einstein--Riemann representation of the broken theory contains an additional generally covariant interaction, making the Weyl-invariant model a particular limit of a more general gravitational theory.

After the launch of Einstein Probe (EP), pulsars are observed to calibrate the timing system of the onboard Follow-up X-ray Telescope (FXT) by measuring the times of arrival (TOAs). For scientific observations, each pnCCD has three distinct readout modes that differ in frame cycles, the full frame mode (FF, 50 ms per frame), the partial windowed mode (PW, 2.2 ms per frame), and the timing mode (TM, 23.68 $μ$s per row, 9.4352 ms per frame). Based on measurements of PSR B1821--24 and PSR B1937+21, the preliminary absolute timing accuracy is estimated to be 6 $μ$s for FXTA and 31 $μ$s for FXTB in TM mode. Additionally, the systematic delay of FXTA timing results relative to FXTB is found to be $20^{\pm19(stat)}_{\pm21(sys)} μ$s.

The young Crab-like pulsar PSR B0540--69, located in the Large Magellanic Cloud, underwent a spin-down rate transition (SRT) in 2011 December, after which its pulsar wind nebula (PWN) was reported to brighten up to 2018. In this work, we extend the study of its X-ray and $γ$-ray flux evolution through 2026 by combining observations from multiple X-ray instruments with data from the Fermi Large Area Telescope, thereby constructing a flux history of the PSR$+$PWN system spanning more than two decades. We find that the enhanced X-ray luminosity of the PSR$+$PWN system has persisted for more than 14 yr after the SRT, up to early 2026, whereas the pulsed X-ray flux remains unchanged across all epochs.
