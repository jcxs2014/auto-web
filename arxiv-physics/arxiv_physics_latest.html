The Standard Model does not contain sufficient amount of CP violation to account for the observed baryon asymmetry of the universe. Beyond Standard Model scenarios that aim to explain this asymmetry introduce new sources of CP violation, but these generically feed into electric dipole moments (EDMs), which are severely constrained by experiment. We indentify a new class of CP-violating sources that cannot generate EDMs directly: EDM operators are C conserving and P violating, whereas CP violation introduced here is, by construction, C violating and P conserving. We give a concrete model realisation accommodating such sources and discuss their relevance for baryogenesis.

标准模型不包含足够数量的 CP 破坏来解释观测到的宇宙重子不对称性。除了旨在解释这种不对称性的标准模型场景之外，还引入了 CP 破坏的新来源，但这些通常会影响电偶极矩 (EDM)，而电偶极矩受到实验的严重限制。我们确定了一类新的不能直接生成 EDM 的 CP 破坏源：EDM 算子是 C 守恒和 P 破坏，而这里引入的 CP 破坏通过构造是 C 破坏和 P 守恒。我们给出了适应这些来源的具体模型实现，并讨论了它们与重子发生的相关性。

Modern high energy nuclear and particle physics experiments produce large amounts of sparse data that must be reduced significantly in size before performing physics analysis. Recently, there has been an effort to evaluate Foundation Models as a potential tool for performing data analysis. These models have been shown to learn features in high dimensional datasets based on the multi-modal data they are trained on. To show their effectiveness at performing complex pattern recognition tasks, such as reconstructing charged particles in a magnetic field, we evaluated the performance of a Foundation Model based reconstruction on several downstream tasks with a dataset composed of spacepoints in a Time Projection Chamber. We show that the performance of the Foundation Model based data analysis significantly improves upon that of traditional methods, and furthermore can be adapted to learn additional tasks that are not necessarily related to the initial training objective.

We determine the surface-defect difference operators of the 4d $\mathcal{N}=2$ lens space index for class $\mathcal{S}$ theories of type $A_{N-1}$ on $(\mathbb{S}^3/\mathbb{Z}_r)\times\mathbb{S}^1$. For generic fugacities $(p,q,t)$ and arbitrary rank $N$, orbifold order $r$ and discrete holonomies, their coefficients are finite products of theta functions with nomes $p^r$ and $q^r$. After an explicit gauge transformation, Gaussian in the holonomies, each operator factorizes into two elliptic Ruijsenaars--Schneider-type operators with step $pq$, one with nome $p^r$ and one with nome $q^r$, acting on variables glued along a lattice. Using this factorization and a Wronski-type relation, we prove that all the operators commute. We also establish a sufficient criterion for self-adjointness with respect to the bilinear vector multiplet pairing. It applies to the basic mixed operator and, in suitable parameter chambers, to the pure $p$- and $q$-type operators; for general mixed operators the contour prescription remains open. These results support the expectations from S-duality.

我们确定 $(\mathbb{S}^3/\mathbb{Z}_r)\times\mathbb{S}^1$ 上 $A_{N-1}$ 类型 $\mathcal{S}$ 类理论的 4d $\mathcal{N}=2$ 透镜空间索引的表面缺陷差分算子。对于一般逸度 $(p,q,t)$ 和任意秩 $N$、轨道阶 $r$ 和离散完整函数，它们的系数是 theta 函数与 nomes $p^r$ 和 $q^r$ 的有限乘积。经过显式规范变换（全称中的高斯变换），每个算子分解为两个椭圆 Ruijsenaars——步长为 $pq$ 的施耐德型算子，一个带有 nome $p^r$，一个带有 nome $q^r$，作用于沿着格子粘合的变量。使用这种因式分解和 Wronski 型关系，我们证明所有运算符都是可交换的。我们还建立了关于双线性向量多重配对的自伴性的充分标准。它适用于基本混合算子，并且在合适的参数室中，适用于纯$p$-和$q$-类型算子；对于一般混合算子，轮廓处方保持开放。这些结果支持了 S-对偶性的期望。

We develop a topological and combinatorial interpretation of the Kawai--Lewellen--Tye (KLT) relations for $n$-point AdS string-amplitude building blocks, which are disk integrals with multivariable multiple polylogarithm insertions and sphere integrals with their single-valued counterparts. Building on the framework of twisted de Rham theory over a noncommutative ring, we reformulate the problem in terms of associahedral chambers of the moduli space loaded with solutions of the Knizhnik--Zamolodchikov equation. We construct regularized twisted cycles and express their intersection pairings through local monodromies and Drinfeld associators, which control transport between fusion-tree normalizations. Organizing the intersection contributions by common boundary faces yields the intersection matrix, which is identified with the inverse KLT kernel by the twisted period relation. The field-theory limit of the inverse KLT kernel is also given.

Seven-dimensional supergravity poses a uniquely intricate supersymmetry problem, combining topologically massive higher-form gauge dynamics, distinct Clifford algebra properties, and rich geometric structures. We first establish the complete formulation of the maximal Cartan-gauged theory in terms of the gauge-invariant higher-form flux, including both the bosonic action and supersymmetry variations. This formulation, which also makes the smooth ungauged limit manifest, is achieved by requiring a consistent truncation to the minimal Cartan-gauged theory in the equal-charge limit. We construct the complete bilinear system for a timelike Killing vector, where additional internal orientation of the symplectic-Majorana spinor arises already in the scalar sector, uniquely among pure minimal ungauged theories. In the equal-charge sector, we decouple the algebraic constraints into SU$(3)$-isotypic components, and analyse the differential bilinear relations, determining the permitted dilaton gradient, fluxes, fibration twist and intrinsic torsion of the base. We prove that with gauging and active Cartan flux, the complex scalar bilinear vanishes identically. We also construct a Killing gauge for the higher-form potential compatible with the self-duality condition. We show that with gauging but no active Cartan flux, the Killing vector also acts as a generator of the gauged R-symmetry, and the base structure is enlarged from SU$(3)$ to U$(3)$. We find that BPS-flow solutions realize this setting. With supersymmetry, we isolate a single independent component of the 3-form equation across an exhaustive four-class partition for both theories within this setup. Applied to known and proposed solutions in both theories, this classification exposes unexplored sectors and provides an accessible toolbox for constructing and testing solutions and developing new geometric and holographic applications.

七维超引力提出了一个独特复杂的超对称问题，结合了拓扑大规模的更高形式的规范动力学、独特的克利福德代数性质和丰富的几何结构。我们首先根据规范不变的高形式通量建立了最大嘉当规范理论的完整公式，包括玻色子作用和超对称变化。该公式也使平滑的非计量极限变得明显，它是通过要求对等电荷极限中的最小嘉当计量理论进行一致截断来实现的。我们为类时 Killing 向量构建了完整的双线性系统，其中辛马约拉纳旋量的附加内部方向已经出现在标量扇区中，这在纯最小非规范理论中是独一无二的。在等电荷部分，我们将代数约束解耦为 SU$(3)$ 同型分量，并分析微分双线性关系，确定允许的膨胀梯度、通量、纤维化扭曲和基底的固有扭转。我们证明，通过计量和主动嘉当通量，复标量双线性同样消失。我们还构建了一个与自我二元性条件兼容的更高形式潜力的杀伤量表。我们表明，在计量但没有主动嘉当通量的情况下，杀伤矢量也充当计量 R 对称性的生成器，并且基础结构从 SU$(3)$ 扩大到 U$(3)$。我们发现 BPS-flow 解决方案实现了这一设置。通过超对称性，我们在该设置中的两种理论的详尽四类划分中隔离了 3 型方程的单个独立分量。该分类适用于两种理论中已知和提出的解决方案，揭示了未探索的领域，并提供了一个可访问的工具箱，用于构建和测试解决方案以及开发新的几何和全息应用。

Magnetic null points define the magnetic field topology in stellar coronae, planetary magnetospheres, and astrospheres, and are commonly associated with magnetic reconnection. We investigate the local structure of stationary three-dimensional (3D) resistive, incompressible magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) flows in the vicinity of an isolated magnetic null and an isolated velocity stagnation point, assuming that the two nulls are colocated. The governing MHD equations are solved self-consistently using a first-order local expansion of the magnetic and velocity fields about the null. For any given magnetic-null configuration, the equations admit a two-parameter family of stationary solutions, comprising multiple solution branches with distinct velocity topologies. Along these families, the velocity-null polarity can change at bifurcation points where the flow becomes effectively 2.5D. A single magnetic null can therefore support positive-radial, negative-radial, and spiral stagnation flows, with qualitatively different inflow, outflow, and rotational properties. The associated pressure and resistivity distributions are likewise determined self-consistently and vary across the solution family, demonstrating that they are coupled to the flow topology rather than prescribed independently. The generic solutions do not require the velocity and magnetic separatrices to coincide, allowing plasma to cross the magnetic fan separatrix and providing a local reconnective flow. These results demonstrate that, in the presence of nonuniform resistivity, the magnetic topology alone does not uniquely determine the associated stationary flow, pressure, or resistivity structure around a 3D magnetic null, revealing the nonlinear feedback between all macroscopic variables and resistivity. The constant-resistivity field-aligned and spiral field-crossing solutions identified previously are recovered as special cases.

We present the design methodology, implementation, and data products of the Energetic Particle Detector (EPD) instrument on-board the Electron Losses and Fields Investigation (ELFIN) dual SmallSat mission. ELFIN was designed to study the time-evolution of energetic particle distributions in Earth's magnetosphere, having operated in polar Low Earth Orbit (LEO) from 2018 to 2022. Each satellite's instrument payload contained a pair of detector heads to sense differential-directional fluxes of 50 keV to $\sim$5 MeV electrons (EPDE), and 50 keV to $\sim$2 MeV atomic ions (EPDI), with an energy resolution better than 50% ($ΔE/E<0.5$). The satellites were spinning with a nominal period of 2.8 s in a plane containing Earth's background magnetic field, with particle counting intervals subdivided into 16 consecutive spin sectors. Each EPD was paired with a three-axis Fluxgate Magnetometer (FGM) sampled at 80 Hz for local pitch-angle determination. Each detector head operated on the principle of pulse-height analysis (PHA) in solid state media, collimated by a rectangular 22.5$^\circ$ field of view and entrance aperture. The EPDE contained a stack of 6 silicon detectors (0.7 cm total thickness) with 0.15 cm$^2$ str geometric factor, with ion contamination mitigated by an aperture foil and digital coincidence logic. The EPDI contained a stack of 2 silicon detectors (0.1 cm total thickness) with 0.03 cm$^2$ str geometric factor, with electron contamination mitigated by a magnetic broom stage and digital anti-coincidence logic. The total assembled mass, volume, and power required for the EPD detector heads and circuit boards were 939 g, 10$\times$9.8$\times$8.4 cm$^3$, and 2.3 W, respectively. We describe each EPD design element alongside instrument data products and processing.
