We study a real scalar field nonminimally coupled to curvature on a fixed Ellis wormhole background. The scalar perturbation decouples at linear order, and the substitution $ x=a \tan z $ reduces its static radial equation to a trigonometric Pöschl-Teller problem. This yields the exact threshold spectrum $ ζ_{n\ell}=(n+\ell+1)^2/2 $, closed-form Gegenbauer profiles, and an exact count of negative radial eigenvalues in each angular sector. The s-wave thresholds are zero-energy resonances at which the growth rate rises linearly, whereas the $ \ell \geq1\ $ threshold states are square integrable and the growth rate rises as a square root. At the first threshold we also obtain the next-order term, $ σa=(π/4)g-(3π/16)g^2+O(g^3) $ with $g=2ζ-1 $. For a probe field with quartic self-interaction, we show that no nontrivial spherical static solution exists for $ζ\leq1/2 $ and derive local branch expansions near the s-wave thresholds. Numerical evolutions are consistent with saturation on the nodeless branch. These nonlinear results refer to the probe-field model; they do not establish the existence or stability of fully backreacted scalarized wormholes.

In this note we present a detailed computation of the BPS states from M2-branes wrapping 2-cycles in local Calabi-Yau manifolds, at the level of brane quantization. For the cases of local Calabi-Yau threefolds, the supersymmetric quantum mechanics is the 1d $\mathcal{N}=4$ non-linear sigma model. We achieve systematic, physical interpretations of refined Gopakumar-Vafa (GV) invariants, first for the curve classes with fixed geometric genus, and then for curves whose geometric genus jumps. The BPS states of 5d $\mathcal{N}=1$ superconformal field theories are interpreted from the local geometries, which are presented in a number of case studies. In particular, we compute the refined GV invariants for generalized conifolds, local CY3 giving 5d $\mathcal{N}=1$ gauge theories and a singularity whose crepant resolution contains a singular divisor. For the cases of local Calabi-Yau fourfolds, we point out the supersymmetric quantum mechanics is of type $\mathcal{N}=2B$. We set up the framework for studying M2-branes wrapping 2-cycles, and discuss a few cases of M2-brane wrapping genus-0 curves with this new approach.

We study the large-$N$ asymptotics of ratios of Lukyanov integrals for fixed real coupling $b\geq1$. Our approach uses their biorthogonal ensemble structure and vector Riemann--Hilbert techniques. Under stated analytic hypotheses, we derive the logarithmic ratio through its constant term, which is expressed by a Fredholm determinant. The expansion is locally uniform in the real parameter $α$ and is formulated separately for even and odd particle numbers. We verify the hypotheses for the correction defined by the physical ground-state thermodynamic Bethe ansatz. The relation to quantum-field-theoretic one-point functions is discussed separately.

We study the real-time response of a Schwarzschild black hole in the Hartle-Hawking state to an external massless scalar field. Using a complexified radial contour to represent the Schwinger-Keldysh closed time path, we describe dissipation, thermal fluctuations and conservative tidal response within a common framework. Before performing tidal matching, we derive the equilibrium fluctuation-dissipation relation directly from the radial construction for every scalar multipole at arbitrary real frequency. In the low-frequency tidal expansion, we recover the known relation between the logarithmic running of the conservative dynamical Love coefficient at order $ω^2$ and the leading dissipative response at order $ω$. We explain how the same leading absorptive coefficient enters both relations at low frequency and trace the unit coefficient of the running relation to the Schwarzschild horizon slope. In the Hartle-Hawking state, this common coefficient relates the logarithmic running directly to the zero-frequency thermal noise. Kubo-Martin-Schwinger analyticity determines the thermal noise from the dissipative response, whereas the near-zone radial equation and tidal matching determine the conservative logarithmic running.

We derive the parallel-transport holonomies of circles centered on the symmetry axis in the homogeneous Gödel-type family of Rebouças and Tiomno, which includes the Gödel universe, the causal member with $m^2=4Ω^2$, and the Som--Raychaudhuri limit. The holonomy is governed by a single invariant $Δ(r)$, whose sign determines whether the Lorentz transformation is elliptic, parabolic, or hyperbolic. We determine the number of parabolic radii across the family and prove that, whenever the azimuthal circles become closed timelike curves (CTCs), the chronology radius satisfies $Δ(r_c)=-\cosh(mr_c)<0$. We also express this invariant in terms of Killing-vector scalars, derive the vector and spinor Wilson loops, give the exact condition for trivial holonomy after a finite number of turns, and analyze Fermi--Walker transport on circular CTCs. Finally, we discuss an analogy with the effective metric of a rotating superfluid, valid under stated conditions, and derive exact expressions for the radial $L^2$ localization of Klein--Gordon Landau modes relative to the Som--Raychaudhuri chronology radius. The geometric results are exact within the metric family considered; the discussion of the analog system relies on the validity of a coarse-grained effective description.

We study the Wilsonian exact renormalization group flow of the gauged Nambu--Jona-Lasinio (NJL) model, a four-dimensional $U(1)$ gauge theory with chirally invariant four-Fermi interactions, by using the gradient flow exact renormalization group~(GFERG). Our gauge-invariant ansatz (truncation) for the 1PI Wilson action keeps a general photon kinetic function~$\mathcal{K}_τ(k^2)$, the fermion mass, and the scalar and vector four-Fermi couplings. For a fixed gauge coupling~$e^2$, the Gaussian and NJL (quasi-)fixed points on the critical surface merge at~$e_c^2\simeq17$, as anticipated by the ladder Schwinger--Dyson equation and the conventional Wilsonian renormalization group. We then let $e^2$ itself flow. Owing to the manifest gauge invariance of the GFERG, the flow of~$e^2$ is governed solely by the photon anomalous dimension~$γ_A$, which remains positive along both branches up to the merging point. Hence, no fixed point with~$e^2\neq0$ exists. Flows from a sufficiently large~$e^2$ in the ultraviolet (UV) reach the chirally broken phase even for vanishing four-Fermi couplings, so the gauge interaction does trigger chiral symmetry breaking. The merging point, however, is not a fixed point, and the gauge coupling vanishes in any continuum limit within our truncation.

我们通过使用梯度流精确重正化群~(GFERG)，研究了规范的 Nambu--Jona-Lasinio (NJL) 模型的威尔逊精确重整化群流，这是一种具有手性不变四费米相互作用的四维 $U(1)$ 规范理论。我们的 1PI Wilson 作用的规范不变 ansatz（截断）保留了一般光子动力学函数 ~$\mathcal{K}_τ(k^2)$、费米子质量以及标量和矢量四费米耦合。对于固定规范耦合 ~$e^2$，临界面上的高斯和 NJL（准）固定点在 ~$e_c^2\simeq17$ 处合并，正如阶梯 Schwinger-Dyson 方程和传统威尔逊重正化群所预期的那样。然后我们让 $e^2$ 本身流动。由于 GFERG 明显的规范不变性，~$e^2$ 的流动仅受光子反常维度~$γ_A$ 控制，该维度沿着两个分支直到合并点都保持正值。因此，不存在 ~$e^2\neq0$ 的不动点。即使对于消失的四费米耦合，来自足够大的〜$e^2$的紫外（UV）流也会达到手性破缺相，因此规范相互作用确实会触发手性对称性破缺。然而，合并点不是固定点，并且规范耦合在我们的截断范围内的任何连续统极限中消失。

TianQin, a space-borne gravitational wave observatory, is designed to detect gravitational-wave signals via laser interferometry. As the inhomogeneous space plasma surrounds the inter-satellite laser links, the propagation of the laser in space plasma can introduce pointing error noise. In this work, we quantitatively assess the effect of plasma on the pointing accuracy of TQ's inter-satellite laser links. We employ a magnetohydrodynamic model to simulate the plasma distribution around the TQ orbit for different orbital orientations. Using the fourth-order Runge-Kutta method to conduct the ray-tracing calculations over a TQ orbital cycle under a solar wind dynamic pressure close to the 23-year average, we find that the plasma-induced laser deflection angle and pointing eccentricity are on the order of $10^{-20} \ \mathrm{rad}$ and picometers, respectively. These values are significantly lower than TQ's requirements. Our results demonstrate that the impact of space plasma on TQ's pointing error is negligible in moderate space weather conditions, indicating a positive outlook for the TQ mission.

We use the core-radius, $r_γ,$ of a galaxy's central core along with a volume-limited sample of brightest cluster galaxies (BCG) to determine the $z=0$ mass distribution function of the most massive super-massive black holes (SMBH). We recover a $M_{BH}-r_γ$ relationship between the SMBH mass, $M_{BH},$ and $r_γ$ of form $M_{BH} \approx1.3\times10^{10}~(r_γ/{\mathrm{kpc}})~M_\odot,$ with $σ= 0.25\pm0.03$ dex scatter in $M_{BH}.$ We derive a core distribution function (CDF), based on HST and DLS images of the BCGs. The CDF falls steeply as $N(r_γ)\sim r_γ^{-3}$ for $r_γ>0.50$ kpc. Only $\sim5\%$ of BCGs have $r_γ>1$ kpc. Combining the CDF and $M_{BH} - r_γ$ relationship yields the BH mass distribution function (MDF). For $M_{BH} \geq 7\times10^{9}~\mathrm{M_\odot}$ (the mean $M_{BH}$ at $r_γ=0.50$ kpc) we derive: $\log\left(\frac{dN(M_{BH})}{d{M_{BH}/10^{10} M_\odot}}~[\rm Mpc^{-3}]\right)=(-3.05\pm0.42)~\log\left(\frac{M_{BH}}{10^{10}M_\odot}\right)~-~5.11\ (+0.41, -0.36)$. Cores with $r_γ\geq 0.50$ kpc are found almost exclusively in BCGs, implying that SMBHs with $M_{BH} \geq7\times10^{9}~\mathrm{M_\odot}$ may also be exclusive to BCGs.
